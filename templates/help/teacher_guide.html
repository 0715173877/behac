Teacher Guide

As a Teacher, you can record results, take attendance, manage assignments, and track pupil behavior. This guide shows you how.

1. Getting Started

1
Log In

Go to the login page and enter your username and password provided by the school administrator.

2
Teacher Dashboard

After logging in, you'll see your Teacher Dashboard showing your classes, today's timetable, recent results, and upcoming assignments.

3
Navigate the Sidebar

Use the sidebar on the left to access Academics (Results, Attendance, Assignments, Exams, Timetable, Subjects, Classes) and Records (Pupils, Behavior, Achievements).

2. Recording Results

1
Go to Results

Click Results in the sidebar under "Academics".

2
Add a New Result

Click Add Result. Select the pupil, subject, exam, and enter the score. You can also upload results in bulk using a CSV file.

3
Bulk Upload

Click Bulk Upload to upload multiple results at once using a CSV file. Download the sample template first to ensure correct formatting.

Tip: Results are visible to parents in their dashboard, so keep them up to date after each exam or test.

3. Taking Attendance

1
Go to Attendance

Click Attendance in the sidebar under "Academics".

2
Record Attendance

Click Add Attendance. Select the class, date, and mark each pupil as Present (P), Absent (A), or Late (L).

3
View Attendance History

The attendance list shows all records. You can filter by class, date, or pupil to review attendance patterns.

4. Managing Assignments

1
Go to Assignments

Click Assignments in the sidebar under "Academics".

2
Add an Assignment

Click Add Assignment. Enter the title, description, select the class, subject, and set a due date. You can also upload a file (e.g., a PDF worksheet).

3
Edit or Delete

Use the edit or delete icons next to any assignment to make changes or remove it.

5. Managing Exams

1
Go to Exams

Click Exams in the sidebar under "Academics".

2
Add an Exam

Click Add Exam. Enter the exam name, select the term, subject, class, and set the date and maximum score.

3
Record Results for Exams

After creating an exam, go to Results to record scores for each pupil who took the exam.

6. Behavior & Achievements

1
Record Behavior

Click Behavior under "Records" to log behavior incidents. Select the pupil, choose the type (Positive/Negative), describe the incident, and set the severity.

2
Award Achievements

Click Achievements under "Records" to award achievements to pupils for good performance, sports, or conduct.

Tip: Parents can see both behavior records and achievements in their dashboard, so use these features to keep them informed.

7. Viewing Pupils

1
Pupils List

Click Pupils under "Records" to view all enrolled pupils. You can search by name or filter by class.

2
Pupil Details

Click on a pupil's name to view their full profile, including personal info, parent details, and class.

8. Viewing Your Timetable

1
Your Timetable

Your current day's timetable is shown on the Teacher Dashboard. Click Timetable in the sidebar to view the full weekly schedule.
