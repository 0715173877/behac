Parent Guide

As a Parent, you can monitor your child's academic progress, payments, behavior, attendance, and timetable — all from your personal dashboard.

1. Getting Started

1
Log In

Go to the login page. Use the email address you registered with as your username. Your default password is parent123 (provided by the school).

2
Change Your Password

After your first login, click Change Password at the bottom of the sidebar to set a new, secure password.

3
Select Your Child

If you have more than one child in the school, use the dropdown at the top of the dashboard to switch between them.

Outstanding Balance: If you have unpaid school fees, you will be blocked from viewing your child's progress, behavior, and other details until the balance is cleared. Please contact the school accountant to make payments.

2. Dashboard Overview

Your main dashboard shows a summary of your child's information at a glance:

Quick Stats

See your child's class, total paid fees, balance, attendance summary (present/absent/late), and recent achievements.

Recent Results

View the most recent exam results and see how your child is performing across subjects.

Upcoming Assignments

See homework and assignments that are due soon so you can help your child stay on track.

Payment Summary

A chart shows payments made throughout the year so you can track what has been paid.

3. Viewing Full Progress

1
Go to Full Progress

Click Full Progress in the sidebar under "My Child".

2
View Results by Term

Results are organized by academic year and term. You can see scores for each subject across different exams.

3
Track Improvement

Compare results across terms to see how your child is improving over time.

4. Viewing Payments

1
Go to Payments

Click Payments in the sidebar under "My Child".

2
View Payment History

See a complete list of all payments made, including dates, amounts, receipt numbers, and fee categories.

3
Check Balance

The top of the page shows your total paid, expected fees, and any outstanding balance.

Tip: If you see an outstanding balance, contact the school accountant to make a payment. Once paid, your access will be restored.

5. Viewing Behavior Records

1
Go to Behavior

Click Behavior in the sidebar under "My Child".

2
View Incidents

See a log of behavior incidents recorded by teachers, including the type (Positive/Negative), description, date, and severity.

3
Positive Recognition

Positive behavior records highlight good conduct, helpfulness, and other commendable actions by your child.

6. Viewing Attendance

1
Go to Attendance

Click Attendance in the sidebar under "My Child".

2
View Attendance Records

See a daily log of your child's attendance — marked as Present (P), Absent (A), or Late (L).

3
Attendance Summary

The dashboard shows a summary of total present, absent, and late days for quick reference.

7. Viewing Timetable

1
Go to Timetable

Click Timetable in the sidebar under "My Child".

2
Weekly Schedule

View your child's weekly class schedule showing subjects, teachers, and time periods for each day.

3
Upcoming Assignments

The timetable page also shows upcoming assignments and their due dates.

8. Viewing Achievements

1
Go to Achievements

Click Achievements in the sidebar under "My Child".

2
View Awards

See all achievements and certificates awarded to your child, including the title, description, and date awarded.

Tip: Celebrate your child's achievements with them! Positive reinforcement encourages good performance and behavior.

9. Troubleshooting

Can't log in?

Contact the school administrator to reset your password or verify your account.

No child linked to my account?

Contact the school administrator to link your child's record to your parent account.

Page says I'm blocked?

This means there is an outstanding fee balance. Contact the school accountant to make a payment and restore your access.
