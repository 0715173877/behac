Accountant Guide

As an Accountant, you manage all financial aspects of the school — recording payments, managing fee structures, and generating reports.

1. Getting Started

1
Log In

Go to the login page and enter your username and password provided by the school administrator.

2
Accountant Dashboard

After logging in, you'll be taken to the Finance Dashboard showing revenue summaries, payment trends, and quick stats.

3
Navigate the Sidebar

Use the sidebar on the left to access Payments, Finance Dashboard, Reports, Outstanding Balances, Fee Categories, and Fee Structures.

2. Recording Payments

1
Go to Payments

Click Payments in the sidebar under "Finance".

2
Record a New Payment

Click Add Payment. Select the pupil, choose the fee category, enter the amount paid, and select the payment method (Cash, Bank, Mobile Money, etc.).

3
Receipt Number

Each payment is automatically assigned a unique receipt number. You can also enter a manual receipt number if needed.

4
Bulk Upload Payments

Click Bulk Upload to record multiple payments at once using a CSV file. Download the sample template to ensure correct formatting.

Tip: Always double-check the pupil name and amount before saving a payment. Once saved, payments can be edited but not deleted without admin approval.

3. Managing Fee Categories

1
Go to Fee Categories

Click Fee Categories in the sidebar under "Finance".

2
Add a Category

Click Add Category. Enter a name (e.g., Tuition, Transport, Lunch, Books) and an optional description.

3
Edit or Delete

Use the edit or delete icons to manage existing categories. Note: you cannot delete a category that has payments or fee structures linked to it.

4. Managing Fee Structures

1
Go to Fee Structures

Click Fee Structures in the sidebar under "Finance".

2
Add a Fee Structure

Click Add Fee Structure. Select the class level, fee category, and enter the amount. This sets the expected fee for each category per class.

3
View by Class

The fee structure list shows all configured fees grouped by class level, making it easy to see what each pupil is expected to pay.

Tip: Set up fee structures at the beginning of each academic year. The system uses these to calculate outstanding balances automatically.

5. Generating Reports

1
Finance Dashboard

Click Finance in the sidebar to view charts and summaries of revenue by month and by category.

2
Payment Reports

Click Reports to generate detailed payment reports. You can filter by date range, class, or pupil.

3
Outstanding Balances

Click Outstanding to see a list of all pupils with unpaid fees. The report shows the expected amount, amount paid, and remaining balance.

Note: Parents with outstanding balances will be blocked from viewing their child's progress, behavior, and other details until the balance is cleared.

6. Viewing Pupil Payment History

1
From Payments List

In the Payments list, you can search by pupil name to see all payments made by a specific pupil.

2
From Pupils List

Click Pupils under "Records" to view a pupil's profile, which includes their payment summary.
